Know the scope. This workspace contains 30 selected papers, not the whole web. Explanations come from 99 stored, source-linked claims; they can be incomplete. Open a citation to inspect the original paper.
The 30-paper starter collection covers retrieval, reranking, generation and evaluation. It is a chosen study scope, not a hardware limit. Every original PDF is included for offline reading.
Why a fixed collection?

A known collection makes source comparisons and repeatable evaluation possible. The assistant does not claim to search all academic literature. Expanding it requires a new source index and checked explanation units; simply dropping a PDF into a folder does not make it answerable.

Score the delivered answer against the original source PDFs. Do not treat a valid citation as factual verification. Use a pseudonym; record your actual relationship to the project.
